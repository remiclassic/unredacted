The game

You are not the reporter who uncovers the conspiracy. You are its newest hire.

UNREDACTED: The Deep State is a single-player noir stealth-strategy game about the fictional secret society that runs Gilded-Age America, and the people it uses to do it.

In-game · The Interview
The Interview

The interview is the first mission.

You sit across the desk from people who officially do not exist. Ten questions follow, starting with the hardest: who do you think we are, and what do you think we do? Wrong answers get you dismissed. Answers that reveal too much end the conversation permanently. The ones you survive shape your profile: Wet Work or Intel.

In-game · The Office
The Office

A respectable front.

A stately Victorian office, chandelier lit, flag in the corner. On the desk: the File Organizer, the Rolodex, the morning Newspaper, the Stock Ticker, Personnel Data, Recruitment and History. This is where the mail arrives and the decisions get made.

Game art
The Headquarters

Build the room behind the room.

Behind the office, dig in. Build a multi-floor secret headquarters (War Room, Think Tank, Communication Room, R&D, Chemistry Lab, Dojo, Armory, Infirmary, Training Grounds, Power Room) and place facilities to earn adjacency bonuses. Keep Power, Supplies and Money flowing. Time moves on its own; speed it up when results can't wait.

In-game · File Organizer
The Network

Information is ammunition.

Recruit a think tank from Industry, the Military, Strategists and the Black Market. Work Wall Street for investment opportunities and the Foreign Embassies for blackmail. Research wiretaps, poisons, communications and hospital equipment. Then read the clues (the basement key is somewhere in the barn) and plan accordingly.

In-game · Tactical pause & action queue
The Operation

Plan. Pause. Execute.

Missions play out top-down in real time, with tactical pause and an action queue. Guards see and hear. Footprints give you away. Fog of war hides what you haven't scouted. Pick locks, ambush, subdue, carry bodies out of sight, distract, confront, then finish the job: kill, kidnap, plant evidence, extract. Optional conditions raise the stakes: don't kill anyone else; don't let anyone flee.

In-game · Combat
The Cost

Every kill leaves a mark.

Your operatives have stats (Stealth, Lockpicking, Sabotage, Close Combat, Persuasion, Sniper and more) and they have mental health. Killing takes a toll on the one who does it and on anyone close enough to see. The cabal asks for results. Your people pay for them.

Questions

For the record.

What is UNREDACTED: The Deep State?

A single-player noir stealth-strategy game set in 1898 America. You're recruited into a fictional secret society, then run it: building a hidden headquarters, gathering intel and leverage, and sending operatives on real-time-with-pause stealth missions.

When is it coming out, and on what platforms?

The release window and platforms haven't been announced yet. The game is in development, and the Steam page is coming soon.

How much will it cost?

Pricing will be announced ahead of release.

Is it based on real history?

It's historical fiction. The setting is real (Gilded-Age America and the run-up to the Spanish-American War) but the cabal and its operations are invented. It's a pulp conspiracy story, not a statement about modern politics.

Can I play without killing?

Missions include non-lethal tools like subduing and distracting, and some objectives require you not to kill anyone else. Killing also has a mental-health cost for your operatives. How far a fully non-lethal approach can go is still being tuned during development.

Will there be a demo, playtest or Early Access?

Not announced yet. Supported languages and Steam Deck compatibility are also still to be decided.
