The official record

Shortly after midnight on August 31, 1997, Diana, Princess of Wales, was fatally injured when the car carrying her crashed in the Pont de l'Alma tunnel in Paris while pursued by photographers. The game

You are not the reporter who uncovers the conspiracy. You are its newest hire.

UNREDACTED: The Deep State is a single-player noir stealth-strategy game about the fictional secret society behind history's headlines, and the people it uses to write them. It begins in 1898. This is the job, in the order you will do it.

01 / 06
  1. Step 01 · The Interview

    The interview is the first mission.

    You sit across the desk from people who officially do not exist. Ten questions follow, starting with the hardest: who do you think we are, and what do you think we do? Wrong answers get you dismissed. Answers that reveal too much end the conversation permanently. The ones you survive shape your profile: Wet Work or Intel.

    10questions. Not all of them can be survived.

  2. Step 02 · The Office

    A respectable front.

    A stately Victorian office, chandelier lit, flag in the corner. On the desk: the File Organizer, the Rolodex, the morning Newspaper, the Stock Ticker, Personnel Data, Recruitment and History. This is where the mail arrives and the decisions get made.

    7instruments on the desk, from the Rolodex to the Stock Ticker.

  3. Step 03 · The Headquarters

    Build the room behind the room.

    Behind the office, dig in. Build a multi-floor secret headquarters and place facilities to earn adjacency bonuses. Keep Power, Supplies and Money flowing. Time moves on its own; speed it up when results can't wait.

    • War Room
    • Think Tank
    • Communication Room
    • R&D
    • Chemistry Lab
    • Dojo
    • Armory
    • Infirmary
    • Training Grounds
    • Power Room

    10facilities to build, three resources to balance.

  4. Step 04 · The Network

    Information is ammunition.

    Recruit a think tank from Industry, the Military, Strategists and the Black Market. Work Wall Street for investment opportunities and the Foreign Embassies for blackmail. Research wiretaps, poisons, communications and hospital equipment. Then read the clues (the basement key is somewhere in the barn) and plan accordingly.

    4circles to recruit from: Industry, Military, Strategists, Black Market.

  5. Step 05 · The Operation

    Plan. Pause. Execute.

    Missions play out top-down in real time, with tactical pause and an action queue. Guards see and hear. Footprints give you away. Fog of war hides what you haven't scouted. Then finish the job: kill, kidnap, plant evidence, extract. Optional conditions raise the stakes: don't kill anyone else; don't let anyone flee.

    • Pick lock
    • Break in
    • Ambush
    • Subdue
    • Grab
    • Carry
    • Distract
    • Confront

    8ways to act, queued while the world stands still.

  6. Step 06 · The Cost

    Every kill leaves a mark.

    Your operatives have stats, and they have mental health. Killing takes a toll on the one who does it and on anyone close enough to see. The cabal asks for results. Your people pay for them.

    −5mental health for the operative who kills. −1 for operatives nearby.

Figures reflect the current pre-alpha build and may change.

Questions

For the record.

Transcript of the questions we are asked most. Answers are complete to the extent permitted.

What is UNREDACTED: The Deep State?

A single-player noir stealth-strategy game about a fictional secret society working behind the headlines, from 1898 to the present day. You're recruited into it, then run it: building a hidden headquarters, gathering intel and leverage, and sending operatives on real-time-with-pause stealth missions.

When is it coming out, and on what platforms?

The release window and platforms haven't been announced yet. The game is in development, and the Steam page is coming soon.

How much will it cost?

Pricing will be announced ahead of release.

Is it based on real history?

It's historical fiction. The events on the timeline are real, and the official records quoted here are accurate. The cabal, its files and its operations are invented. No disrespect is intended to the people who lost their lives or to their families.

Can I play without killing?

Missions include non-lethal tools like subduing and distracting, and some objectives require you not to kill anyone else. Killing also has a mental-health cost for your operatives. How far a fully non-lethal approach can go is still being tuned during development.

Will there be a demo, playtest or Early Access?

Not announced yet. Supported languages and Steam Deck compatibility are also still to be decided.
