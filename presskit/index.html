Description

Short

America, 1898. Survive a lethal job interview, then run the secret cabal behind the headlines. Build a hidden headquarters, trade in intel and blackmail, and send operatives on real-time-with-pause stealth missions to rewrite history. Every kill leaves a mark on the killer.

Long

It is 1898, and the headlines have authors.

UNREDACTED: The Deep State is a single-player noir stealth-strategy game about the secret society that runs America from the shadows, and about the people it uses to do it. You are not the hero who exposes the conspiracy. You are its newest hire.

The Interview. The game opens with a job interview in a dark, lamp-lit hall. "Who do you think we are and what do you think we do?" Ten questions follow. Some answers get you dismissed. One or two reveal that you know too much, and the interview ends permanently. The answers you survive shape your profile, leaning you toward Wet Work or Intel. The last question is less a question than a warning: you can never retire from this job; it will retire you.

The Office and the Headquarters. Your front is a stately Victorian office: a File Organizer, a Rolodex, the morning Newspaper, a Stock Ticker, Personnel Data, Recruitment and History. Behind it, you dig in. The secret headquarters is built floor by floor (War Room, Think Tank, Communication Room, Research and Development, Chemistry Lab, Dojo, Armory, Infirmary, Training Grounds and a Power Room) with adjacency bonuses that reward a smart layout. Power, Supplies and Money keep it running, and time keeps moving; speed it up when you need results.

The Network. Recruit a think tank from Industry, the Military, Strategists and the Black Market. Gather intel. Work Wall Street for investment opportunities and the Foreign Embassies for blackmail. Research wiretaps, poisons, communications and hospital equipment, and train your people. Intel turns into clues that shape how you approach the next job.

The Operation. Missions are real-time top-down stealth with tactical pause and an action queue. Guards see and hear; footprints give you away; fog of war hides what you have not scouted. Pick locks, break in, ambush, subdue, grab, carry bodies, distract, confront. Objectives range from killing or kidnapping a target to planting evidence and getting out, with secondary goals such as "Don't kill anyone else" and "Don't let anyone flee."

The Cost. The current roster (John McAdams, Juanita Dominguez, Logan Blackwood, Lynda Jones and William Freedman) each come with their own stats and their own mental health. Killing takes a toll on the killer and on those nearby.

Features

  • An interview you can fail fatally. Ten questions open the game; wrong answers get you dismissed or silenced for knowing too much.
  • Your answers define you. How you answer shapes your profile, leaning toward Wet Work or Intel.
  • Build the room behind the room. A multi-floor secret headquarters hidden behind a stately office, with adjacency bonuses between facilities.
  • Run the front office. File Organizer, Rolodex, Newspaper, Stock Ticker, Personnel Data, Recruitment and History.
  • Information is ammunition. Intel, Wall Street investments, Foreign Embassy blackmail, research and training; clues feed your mission plans.
  • Plan, pause, execute. Real-time stealth with tactical pause and an action queue; sight, hearing, footprints and fog of war.
  • Many ways to finish the job. Kill, subdue, kidnap, plant evidence and extract, with secondary objectives like "Don't let anyone flee."
  • Every kill leaves a mark. Operatives have stats and mental health; violence wears on the killer and those nearby.

Announcement press release

For immediate release

Think Tank Games announces UNREDACTED: The Deep State, a noir stealth-strategy game set in 1898 America

Survive the interview, build the secret headquarters, and send operatives into the night to rewrite history.

Independent studio Think Tank Games today announced UNREDACTED: The Deep State, a single-player stealth-strategy game in which players are recruited into, and then run, a fictional secret cabal pulling the strings behind Gilded-Age America. The game is currently in development.

UNREDACTED: The Deep State begins with a job interview. Across a desk in a dark, lamp-lit hall, the player answers ten questions that decide whether they are hired, dismissed, or found to know too much. The answers that survive shape the player's profile, leaning toward Wet Work or Intel. The final question doubles as the job description: "You can never retire from this job, it will retire you."

Once hired, players run the operation from a stately Victorian office (File Organizer, Rolodex, Newspaper, Stock Ticker, Personnel Data, Recruitment) while building a multi-floor secret headquarters behind it. Facilities such as the War Room, Think Tank, Chemistry Lab, Dojo, Armory and Infirmary benefit from smart placement through adjacency bonuses, and Power, Supplies and Money must be balanced as time passes. Intel, Wall Street investments, Foreign Embassy blackmail and research into wiretaps and poison all feed the planning of each mission.

Missions play out top-down in real time with tactical pause and an action queue. Guards see and hear, footprints betray intruders, and fog of war hides what has not been scouted. Operatives pick locks, set ambushes, subdue, carry bodies, distract and confront, working toward objectives such as eliminating a target, kidnapping, planting evidence and extracting, often with conditions like "Don't kill anyone else." Each operative has their own stats and mental health, and killing takes a toll on the killer and on those nearby.

Key features

  • The Interview: ten questions; wrong answers end your career, or you.
  • Secret headquarters: a multi-floor base behind a stately office, with adjacency bonuses and three resources.
  • Intel, money and leverage: investments, blackmail, research and training; clues shape mission plans.
  • Real-time-with-pause stealth: sight, hearing, footprints, fog of war and an action queue.
  • Operatives with a conscience: stats plus a mental-health system that tracks the cost of violence.

Availability. Platforms, price and release window will be announced later. Footage shown is from a pre-alpha, work-in-progress build.
